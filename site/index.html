03 / Health & ethics

Our standards.

Supporting health records are available during serious enquiries.

Health screening

DNA panel testing on breeding stock, with hip, elbow, cardiac and patella assessments where applicable.

Responsible pairings

Stable temperament is required. No pairings with unstable temperament, hidden health information or excessive dam use.

Written terms

No placement without written terms. Deposit terms, milestones and refund conditions are documented in writing.

04 / Good to know

Questions.

Do you arrange transport within NZ?

Yes. We coordinate approved transport with a documented handover process.

Do you offer stud services?

Yes, for suitable health-screened females. Include her details, pedigree and current health results when enquiring.

What does each puppy go home with?

Age-appropriate vaccinations, microchip, worming record, starter pack and written care guidance.

Do you accept deposits?

Yes. Deposit terms, milestones and refund conditions are documented in writing.
